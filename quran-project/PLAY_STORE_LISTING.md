### English (≤4000 chars)
```
Al-Quran Al-Sabe'a — a beautiful Mushaf reader featuring the authentic KFGQPC Uthmanic Hafs script, with recitations from 20 world-renowned reciters and integrated memorization tools.

Features:
• Full Mushaf (604 pages) in authentic Uthmanic script — works fully offline.
• 20 verified reciters: Al-Sudais, Al-Shuraim, Al-Minshawi, Al-Husary, Al-Afasy, and more.
• Listen mode: tap any ayah to hear it, with auto-advance to the next verse.
• Memorization mode: mark ayahs you stumble on and track your progress per surah.
• Bookmarks for pages and favorite surahs.
• Smart index for surahs and juz, with fast navigation.
• Four accent themes (green, blue, purple, maroon).
• Adjustable Arabic font size (20–72 pt).
• Optional ornate frame and Tajweed coloring.
• Elegant dark theme — easy on the eyes.
• Fully right-to-left Arabic interface.

Privacy:
We collect no personal data. Your preferences are stored locally on your device only. Audio recitations are streamed from the public Islamic Network CDN when you press play.

May Allah accept it from you.
```
